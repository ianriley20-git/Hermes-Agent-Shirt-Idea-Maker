# Seasonal calendar

Date-driven opportunities to check alongside (not instead of) the
niche/subreddit signals in `daily_scan.md`. Each entry has a nudge
window — the period before/during the date when it's worth actively
surfacing concepts for it. This is a first draft based on common
retail/sports/pop-culture moments — **review and edit freely**, same as
`subreddits.md`/`niche_keywords.md`: add, remove, or adjust dates with
no code change required, the prompt reads this file directly at run
time.

Format: `Name — nudge window — one-line angle note`

- Football season kickoff — Aug 20 - Sep 15 — overlaps the existing
  fantasy football niche, not a separate concept source on its own
- MLB playoffs / World Series — Oct 1 - Nov 5 — not currently covered
  by any fixed niche
- Halloween — Oct 1 - Oct 31
- Thanksgiving / Black Friday shopping chaos — Nov 15 - Nov 29
- Ugly Christmas sweater season — Nov 25 - Dec 25 — this is *why*
  r/ChristmasSweaters goes quiet outside this window; treat the
  subreddit check as gated by this nudge window rather than checking it
  daily year-round
- New Year's resolutions (and abandoning them) — Dec 26 - Jan 15
- Super Bowl — Jan 20 - Feb 10
- March Madness — Mar 10 - Apr 5
- Tax season — Mar 15 - Apr 15
- MLB Opening Day / start of baseball season — Mar 20 - Apr 10
- Graduation season — May 1 - Jun 15
- Summer / Fourth of July — Jun 20 - Jul 5
- Back to school — Aug 1 - Sep 10 — overlaps the existing back to
  school niche, not a separate concept source on its own

If today's date falls in more than one nudge window, surface whichever
is more topical/less already-covered by that day's other signals —
don't force multiple seasonal concepts into one batch just because
windows overlap.
